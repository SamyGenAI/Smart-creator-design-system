# Make the thumbnail promise visible

This skill is the YouTube still (1280×720, also checked at 320×180). `newsletter-thumbnail-designer` is the 420×300 email image. Do not use that canvas for a YouTube thumbnail.

Read the video's actual content, target viewer, title and `DESIGN.md`. Identify one concrete idea, contrast or outcome the video can fulfill. Use authentic supplied faces and screenshots where the brief calls for them.

Sketch a few distinct compositions, then choose the one that reads clearly at small size. Text should add information to the title, not repeat all of it. Do not invent result screenshots, endorsements or emotionally misleading scenes. A number on the thumbnail has to be in `$VAULT/raw/me/proof.md` and shown in the video.

The app has no 1280×720 thumbnail mode. Deliver the concept (text, visual, colour role from `DESIGN.md`, what not to do) into the YouTube draft. A dedicated thumbnail canvas, and video or motion graphics, are **later**. Do not stretch the newsletter thumbnail or an infographic into a fake YouTube still.

Inspect the concept at full and small sizes for readability, cropping, contrast and factual accuracy. Return the concept path, the target dimensions and the fact that upload readiness was not claimed. Thumbnail creation does not upload or publish.
